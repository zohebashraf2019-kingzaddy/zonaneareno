The Club

Downtown Reno's
jungle of Latin nightlife

Step under the marquee, across the red carpet and into Zonanea — the Latin lounge where the music stays loud, the shots keep coming and the dance floor doesn't quit until 4AM.

Every weekend is a new world: La Casa del Perreo weekends, Caribbean Vibes Thursdays, jungle Saturdays and the biggest Thanksgiving Eve party in Reno. Resident and guest Latin DJs keep it moving with reggaetón, perreo intenso, salsa, bachata and the Latin hits you came for.
