The Experience

Where Latin Culture Meets Nightlife

Located in the heart of downtown Reno, Zona Nea Latin Lounge brings together the energy of Latin music, handcrafted cocktails, premium bottle service and immersive entertainment.

From reggaetón and perreo to salsa and bachata, every night celebrates our culture through music, atmosphere and unforgettable experiences.
